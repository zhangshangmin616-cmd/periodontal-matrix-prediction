# Chengdu three-dimensional culture, 24 hours

Li and colleagues at Sichuan University profiled human periodontal ligament cells embedded in PLGA scaffolds at 6, 24 and 72 h under static compression at 25 g/cm². This experiment was absent from the panel-development inputs. Compared with the three RNA-seq sources, the assay, architecture and applied pressure differ. We used only the authors' signed fold changes on sheet B. The public workbook and abstract do not establish the array-level donor variability or whether the reference was time-matched at 24 h. Interpret the values as changes reported by the authors. [Original experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23116693/) · [Publisher's worksheet](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0003996912003494-mmc2.xls)

The 24 h table contains 170 upregulated entries and 201 downregulated entries, with 306 distinct nonempty labels across the two blocks. It reports JAM2 on rows 28 and 96 (3.06- and 2.23-fold), and SNTB1 on row 108 (2.18-fold). Each target has one vote, using the mean of the log ratios for the two JAM2 entries. An additional check of the previously downloaded HGNC registry for former symbols and aliases added no gene to the exact 2/101 overlap. Neither the 371 entries nor the two genes supply biological replicates.

On those two reported genes, the mean absolute error of predicting zero was 1.255 log₂ units. Full transfer from Beijing, Bangkok and Xi'an yielded 0.446, 1.826 and 1.088, respectively. The equal-weight three-study reference yielded 0.939 at full effect and 1.176 at quarter effect. Because changes for 99 genes remain unobserved, the full-panel mean absolute error improvement over zero for the equal-weight full prediction ranges from −0.228 to +0.241. At quarter effect the range is −0.057 to +0.060. Positive gain on selected gene hits cannot establish positive gain across the panel. These are mathematical identification bounds conditional on the reported values, not sampling confidence intervals.
