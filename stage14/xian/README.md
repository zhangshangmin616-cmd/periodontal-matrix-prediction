# Six-hour tensile frequency series in primary periodontal ligament stem cells

The Xi'an study describes three donors, fourth-passage cells, osteogenic medium, 12% cyclic equibiaxial strain, and 6 hours of exposure. Extraction from 0, 0.1, 0.5 and 0.7 Hz arms is mentioned in Methods. The public series links eight runs to one BioSample accession, SAMN16261624. The public experiment titles identify three untreated, two 0.1 Hz, and three 0.5 Hz libraries. They do not establish a donor-to-run pairing or identify a 0.7 Hz run. We used within-arm means and scored both public exposure contrasts separately. They share the three controls and represent one study. The source measurements from Hong Kong use a separate 12-hour culture protocol. Exposure duration, culture preparation, donor pool and primary count quantification differ across laboratories. [Xi'an original study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9259061/) · [ENA Xi'an project](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJNA665587) · [Hong Kong data](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE173891)

Comparing the unscaled Hong Kong mean response with the Xi'an 0.1 Hz contrast gave a gene-rank error of 3.008 versus 2.718 for zero and a panel-mean rank error of 1.397 versus 1.590. Both results persisted on deleting any one Hong Kong donor or any one relevant Xi'an run. This produced 10 overlapping sensitivity comparisons. On the log₂ expression scale the errors were 0.277 versus 0.270 for genes and 0.132 versus 0.164 for the panel. On this scale only 9 of 10 single deletions retained the modest gene-level disadvantage. In the 0.5 Hz contrast both endpoints favoured zero on both scales, including all single deletions. A fixed quarter of the Hong Kong shifts improved gene-rank error by 0.010 in the low-frequency contrast and worsened it by 0.101 in the higher-frequency contrast. Full or quarter transfer from Xi'an raised gene error relative to zero on both scales in Hong Kong. All comparisons use the same 101 targets out of 10,805 shared and unique genes. The full library provided every CPM denominator. The entire ranked background has fixed mean percentile.

Run `python stage14/xian/score.py` and `python stage14/xian/check.py` offline with NumPy. Then run `python stage14/xian/extract.py`. The source count file identifiers and the original sample metadata are included. NCBI counts can differ from the authors' RPKM/DESeq2 pipeline.
