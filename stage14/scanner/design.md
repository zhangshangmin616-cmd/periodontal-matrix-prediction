# Reconstructing the two Osaka arrays from public scanner exports

This is a retrospective source audit. The preceding release scored the one deposited sample table whose ID-to-value pairing exactly matched the attached scanner export. The other sample table did not reconcile probe-by-probe. Three published marker ratios were examined to check whether both scanner exports could be independently oriented; these markers will not be used to fit a coefficient or select evaluation genes.

Before inspecting performance of the combined array record, retain the same 101 targets and the Hong Kong 12-hour average of the five donors' measured log2(CPM + 1) changes. Read both original scanner exports, map exact scanner probe IDs to GPL1291 and unambiguous HGNC genes and require positive normalized background-subtracted median intensities in both channels on both arrays for a probe. Use the deposited normalized log2 ratio with its sign verified against published stretch-to-control ratios. For each gene, average the per-probe per-array log ratios with equal weight. Score the shared probes on each array separately, as well as their mean; evaluate all targets with an eligible probe. As a sensitivity report all platform-mapped probes with finite ratios on both scans without the intensity requirement. For interpretation, report row/key agreement and between-array association across matching probes. These are reverse-labelled hybridizations, with the number of independent donors for this material unavailable.

Compare Hong Kong and Osaka at the group level in both directions. Score zero change, the full per-gene source response, a fixed quarter of it and a uniform prediction equal to the source panel mean. Report gene MAE and absolute mean signed error on the exact same genes. These studies differ in time, loading waveform and measurement platform. For the log-scale gene-loss endpoint the distance of the two full group-response vectors must be identical in both directions. Avoid selecting an array, a signal threshold or a direction for the sign of the result.
