# Two-colour Osaka array source audit

Each Osaka specimen accession includes a two-channel scanner export with stable spot identifiers. The two hybridizations reverse the reported Cy3/Cy5 labelling of untreated and stretched cells. The processed sample tables describe the ratio with stretched signal in the numerator for each accession. We recovered the two scanner log-ratio columns using exact probe identifiers and checked the direction against fold changes printed in panel 1a of the primary paper. At each spot the two logged values received equal weight. At genes with several eligible probes, all such probes received equal weight. These hybridizations do not furnish independent donor replicates. [48-hour study](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M109.097303) · [GEO processed and scanner files](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE19118) · [Published Figure 1a, archive PDF page 10](https://mhlw-grants.niph.go.jp/system/files/2010/103071/201024074A/201024074A0004.pdf#page=10)

On the 30,336 platform probes, the processed log ratio matched its scanner value by ID for 963 probes in GSM474118 and all 30,336 in GSM474198. We tabulated all 12 nonblank symbols printed in Figure 1a. Of these, four matched a unique platform probe by the printed symbol and three had reconstructed fold ratios within 1% of the printed value. The fourth was CNTFR (5.88 versus 6.32). The remaining printed symbols were ambiguous or absent under this particular match. The orientation check does not resolve all processing differences. Across all 18,162 probes with positive normalized background-corrected signals in both channels on both scans, the two scanner log ratios had Spearman correlation −0.086. Interpret the combined forecast comparison as a sensitivity to measurement and probe handling.

Among 78 co-detected fixed targets, the unscaled five-donor Hong Kong 12-hour response gave a gene MAE of 0.500 on the two-scan Osaka average, versus 0.482 for zero. Reversing source and destination gave 0.500 versus 0.130. In the forward direction a fixed quarter gave 0.48145 compared with 0.48205 for zero. Including all available finite ratios irrespective of signal gave 95 targets and a quarter-model error of 0.48482 versus 0.48253 for zero. The forecast uses log₂(CPM + 1) changes from RNA sequencing and normalized log ratios from an array. These scores describe the corresponding measured effect scales.
