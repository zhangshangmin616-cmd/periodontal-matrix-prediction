# Transferring tensile responses between human periodontal ligament culture studies

One prediction trained on primary cultures from Hong Kong (five paired donors, 12 hours of 12% tension) was evaluated on an independent culture study in Osaka (48 hours of cyclic tension). The complementary test used the Osaka gene shifts to predict Hong Kong measurements. Hong Kong effects are changes in log₂(CPM + 1). Osaka effects are normalized log₂ fluorescence ratios. Both quantify loading relative to an untreated culture, but they have different dynamic ranges and are measured at different times. Model development had already used the separate outcomes before the cross-study comparison. [Lin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/ejo/cjab043) · [Fujihara et al.](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M109.097303)

This module keeps the earlier prespecified positive-intensity gate and the one array whose processed measurements match the attached scanner file probe by probe (GSM474198). It takes group means before computing errors. The 84 targets had acceptable signal in both channels and a measurable counterpart in Hong Kong. The complete comparison comprises 7,928 mutually measured background genes including the targets. The file with all published measurements also includes weak signals and repeated zero readings.

| Destination for the 84 targets | Zero | Source mean assigned to every gene | Direct measured gene changes | One quarter of those changes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Osaka 48 h, from Hong Kong | 0.8837 | 0.8818 | 0.8801 | 0.8770 |
| Hong Kong 12 h, from Osaka | 0.1320 | 0.1308 | 0.8801 | 0.2565 |

Numbers are mean absolute errors across genes on the respective log₂ readouts. Fifty-two of 84 nonzero changes agreed in direction (Spearman ρ = 0.171). The full-transfer error is the same distance between the two vectors in each direction. Zero-change errors depend on the destination. The gain over zero of direct transfer to the Osaka array was 0.0036 log₂ units. Including all 95 published, mappable targets reversed this gain (errors of 0.7644 for direct transfer and 0.7499 for zero). Across the complete shared background of 7,928 genes the 12-to-48-hour transfer also had larger error than zero (0.8473 and 0.8335). Direct and quarter-scale transfers from Osaka had larger individual-gene error than zero in all five Hong Kong donors. The array has no donor-resolved replication. [GSE19118](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE19118) · [GSE173891](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE173891)

For the independent 72-hour Vienna experiment, 40 targets had published effect sizes from a differential-expression-selected table. Using the full Hong Kong gene effects gave an error of 0.5383 there, versus 0.5258 for zero. Across the 101 targets the mean gain over zero could range from −0.0812 to +0.0713 log₂ units given the 61 unreported effects. The same calculation for quarter-scale effects gave −0.0190 to +0.0192. These are mathematical bounds conditional on the published coefficients. The source mean for the constant comparator was calculated from all 101 Hong Kong genes before inspecting coverage in Vienna. [Janjić et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejcb.2023.151319)

Run `python stage14/stretch/score.py` (NumPy) followed by `python stage14/stretch/check.py` (Python standard library). Inputs are the archived `stage13/external/paired_counts.npz`, `stage13/external/chip_effects.tsv` and `stage12/culture/culture_readouts.tsv`. The checker uses the independently generated `stage13/external/hongkong_target_effects.tsv` to verify the target-panel results. The upstream archive includes original source URLs, checksums, sample mapping, scanner reconciliation and code for rebuilding the compact data from public material. The reproducible outputs are `group_scores.tsv`, `donor_scores.tsv`, `associations.tsv`, `common_target_effects.tsv` and `vienna_bounds.tsv`. All studies, participants, donors and gene rows retain their own units of analysis.
