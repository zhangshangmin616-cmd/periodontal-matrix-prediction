# Transfer of measured tensile responses between independent periodontal culture studies

The source studies and atlas comparisons were already known. This retrospective rule was recorded before the cross-study scores were calculated. The primary comparison joins the unfiltered 12-hour Hong Kong RNA sequencing study (GSE173891) and the 48-hour Osaka two-colour study (GSE19118). Use the preceding probe-level audit and its positive-signal gate for GSM474198. The published orientation must be checked against GEO channel labels and independently interrogated via scanner/author records. Only report an interpretable orientation; a genuine unresolved conflict must be shown using both signs. Test the previously archived, more permissive all-published-value gate separately.

Use the 101 fixed genes where measurable in both studies. Also score the common measured background with no further response filters. For Hong Kong form the average of the five donors' paired log2(CPM + 1) changes with donors equally weighted. For Osaka use the probe-aggregated normalized tensile-to-control log2 effect. Predict each group's measured target from the other using zero, the source panel mean applied to every measured target, directly transferred gene changes and one quarter of those gene changes. The source mean is recomputed for the specified common set. For both directions report absolute error of the panel mean, mean absolute gene error, numerical gain over zero, Spearman correlation (average ranks) and sign agreement for nonzero effects. For transfer to Hong Kong also score each of its five donors separately as a secondary check. An error in one group contrast does not have donor-level replication in Osaka. Reciprocal full-size group predictions have identical absolute distance but can have different gain relative to zero.

For the 72-hour Vienna study use the fixed Hong Kong effects to predict the 40 target coefficients in the selected published table and calculate best/worst full-panel gain over zero for the missing 61 genes. Compare zero, gene-wise full/quarter effects and the constant source mean over all 101. In the reverse direction full-panel information is incomplete at source. Do not impute missing outcomes, fit slopes, choose times by performance, or treat missing genes as zero. The Vienna group coefficients, Osaka fluorescence ratios and estimated RNA-seq changes are distinct measurement operators. Published log-ratios enable descriptive cross-assay comparisons, not a common absolute expression assay. These are comparisons of independent human cell-culture studies at different times, not replications at day 7 in human roots.
