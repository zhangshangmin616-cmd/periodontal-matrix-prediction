# Baseline expression and matched gene panels in five paired cultures

All 101 matrix targets have at least one read among the ten paired libraries. Of these, 97 have at least 1 CPM in three of the five untreated libraries. The average held-out donor gene-rank error for these 97 falls from 1.990 for zero change to 1.674 for prediction by the four other donors. Every held-out donor gains at the gene level. Panel-mean error increases from 0.298 to 0.354 points. Selecting the scored genes using only the four training controls retains 94–96 genes across folds. The gain over zero is +0.314 for individual genes and −0.046 for the panel mean. This demonstrates that genes with scant baseline expression do not explain the observed reversal.

Across all 10,805 matched genes the mean individual-gene rank error falls from 2.370 to 1.962, with an improvement in every held-out donor. For the 80 targets outside the strongest 21 training shifts per fold, average rank gain is +0.063 points across donors. The training-selected 21 genes show a substantially larger gain of +1.213.

Among 10,000 same-size panels of non-matrix genes matched to the baseline expression distribution of the 101 targets, 9,998 show smaller gene-level rank error for the four-donor forecast than for zero. In 4,661 of those 10,000 draws the mean-rank panel error *increases*. The 101 matrix targets' gene-level gain of +0.302 sits near the reference median (+0.324); the reference median gain in panel accuracy is +0.007, compared with −0.053 for the targets. Matching does not control gene covariance. The counts summarise gene panels conditional on these five paired cultures. They do not supply population inference or an enrichment test.

Run `python stage14/robustness/score.py` and `python stage14/robustness/check.py` with NumPy. The required inputs are stage13/external/paired_counts.npz and stage5/reference/features.tsv. The independent checker calculates ranks and held-out averages independently, recomputes 200 donor and comparator rows, and reconstructs the rank-scale gains for all 10,000 matched panels. Original counts and sample mapping are in the preceding release modules.
