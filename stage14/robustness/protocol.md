# Detectability and gene-panel specificity in the paired tensile culture experiment

Record these rules before computing the new controls. The published Hong Kong source and earlier local results for all 101 matrix targets have already been examined. Use the deposited ten count columns, whole-library totals, unique gene mapping, fixed 101-gene list and frozen 10,805-gene common universe from stage13. Training in each fold uses the four other donors' paired responses. Score the fifth donor in log₂(CPM + 1) change and change in expression percentile relative to the same 10,805-gene universe. Keep a fixed unscaled arithmetic mean per gene; compare with predicting zero, a scalar equal to the training mean of the scored genes, and one quarter of the per-gene prediction. Do not refit an effect scale.

1. Repeat within-donor scoring on the 101 genes as well as those with untreated CPM at least 1 in three or more of the five controls, for a common target set. Repeat using the four training controls only, requiring at least three of four. No loaded sample sets eligibility. Compare identical genes in all models within a fold. Summarise as the mean of the five donors' absolute gene errors and of the five absolute mean signed errors. Also report the number of targets with zero raw counts in all ten libraries.
2. Apply the same model to all 10,805 measured genes, to genes outside the full measurable matrix pathway, and to the broader non-panel set. Whole-universe mean rank is fixed by construction. Omit its aggregate rank comparison.
3. Separately score the fifth of the fixed panel genes with largest absolute training-mean changes and its complement. Use 21 genes (ceiling of 20% of 101), stable gene symbol order for any ties, separately per fold and metric. This is a training-effect size check; the sets vary between folds and scales.
4. Make 10,000 reference panels matching the 101 target genes by the ten strata of average untreated culture CPM, using the available non-matrix genes. Quantile boundaries use the measured universe, ties stay in the same stratum. Draw without replacement within each panel, using seed 20261124 and common panels across donors and metrics. Compute the training-mean comparator and zero outcomes as above, expressed as reduction in error versus zero. Report quantiles and counts of panels with improved gene scores, worse aggregate scores, and joint reversal. Relative frequencies are conditional on the panel construction and sample, not donor-level probabilities. Matching baseline expression cannot match gene covariance or measured treatment responsiveness.
