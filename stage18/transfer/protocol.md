# Transfer of measured human tensile responses across laboratories

This is a retrospective extension of a public-data analysis. The original atlas scores and destination changes were examined in the previous release. Before calculating the new Hong Kong-to-destination scores, fix the source, destinations, estimand, comparators and deletions below. No formal preregistration occurred.

Use the five paired donors in the 12-hour Hong Kong experiment GSE173891. Rank every library on the common 10,805-gene background on a 0–100 scale with midranks for ties. For each gene, subtract each donor’s untreated rank from its stretched rank and average the five changes with equal weight. Transport this vector once at full amplitude to three designated contrast means: Wuhan GSE312900 control-vector cultures after 24 and 48 h versus 0 h; and GSE106887, a 12-hour stretch library versus its sole normal library. Score all three regardless of result. Use the previously fixed 101 target genes. Recompute all ranks on the same shared background for every source, destination and comparator.

Read the archived original NCBI gene-count tables for Wuhan and GSE106887. Sum multiple runs of a Wuhan library before ranking. Average ranks within each condition, then subtract the baseline mean for each gene. Use the previously reconstructed Hong Kong gene-count matrix. Apply the atlas’s unchanged three-day tension multipliers at fixed RNA shares to each destination's baseline expression, rerank, and average its predicted target changes. Score four models on identical measured target changes: zero; the complete Hong Kong 101-gene vector; the Hong Kong target-panel mean repeated over all targets; and the tissue atlas. Compare the absolute mean residual and the mean of the absolute residuals. Report raw errors and gains compared with zero; positive gain means a smaller error. Use sign concordance only for pairs of nonzero measured and predicted changes, with both numerator and denominator. Report the sign of the panel mean by providing both signed means.

For the single-deletion checks, omit each Hong Kong donor in turn and recompute the transported predictors. Separately omit each baseline or exposed Wuhan library and recompute that destination's mean changes and atlas prediction. A destination deletion in GSE106887 would empty one arm and is unavailable. These analyses condition on the assayed cultures; the Wuhan time points share baselines, and the GSE106887 contrast has one library per arm. A comparison across different laboratories, protocols, culture contexts and RNA-processing methods cannot isolate the cause of forecast error. No feature choice, sign reversal, model fitting or magnitude adjustment will use the destination outcomes.
