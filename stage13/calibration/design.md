# Scale of untreated tissue variation in Leuven

Retrospective analysis rule fixed before computing these new comparisons. The original day-7 and day-28 results were already known. Keep the same 101-gene panel, 10,806-gene ranking universe, original libraries, and original untreated and day-7 study groups. We ask how the magnitude of the measured day-7 shift compares with contrasts formed from untreated tissue specimens. This assesses the scale of specimen variation and cannot test cell-group causality.

1. Independently reproduce the panel score for all 63 source specimens from counts and compare with the historical score. Obtain the original day-7 arm means and their difference.
2. Negative control using only the 22 untreated specimens in the distinct day-28 sequencing batch. Enumerate every choice of 3 mandibular and 2 maxillary specimens as a pseudo-baseline, matching the jaw counts of the 5 real day-7 controls. Use the remaining 7 mandibular and 10 maxillary untreated specimens as the comparison arm and weight its jaw means by the day-7 exposed fractions 8/19 and 11/19. This uses complementary sets of 5 and 17 untreated tissue specimens. Report the median absolute difference, central 95% span of signed differences, and the fraction at least as large in magnitude as the real day-7 contrast. Also show the unweighted and equal-jaw specifications as sensitivities.
3. Small same-batch check: use the five day-7 untreated samples. Select one baseline control per jaw, and form a second group from the other three. Average each jaw equally in both subsets. The six possible contrasts give a descriptive check at smaller group sizes. Do not pool them with the separate-batch controls.
4. On all 24 day-7 batch specimens, enumerate every reassignment of 3 mandibular and 2 maxillary specimens to the reference arm, holding the original arm sizes and jaw counts fixed. Compare the absolute observed panel difference to this empirical scale. This mixes exposed and untreated samples and is not an untreated negative control. Report counts as descriptive. The public data lack the complete donor key needed for exchangeability or patient-level randomisation inference. The day-28 batch is also not an untreated sample from the day-7 population.

Keep the three reference distributions distinct. No model re-fitting, gene selection, cutpoint search, or confidence intervals over the enumerations. Archive source identifiers, code and all resulting summaries.
