# Influence of reusing a small source or target clinical series

Following inspection of the main cross-study scores, test their sensitivity to omitting any one source participant or one destination specimen. Fix the two forward clinical gene predictors (full source mean, one quarter source mean) and the two Leuven target times. In forward prediction from Seoul, leave out each one of the 11 source participants in turn, or retain all source participants and drop one treated or untreated Leuven specimen at a time when computing the observed destination contrast. Repeat this one-at-a-time diagnostic for the reciprocal prediction from Leuven day 7 to Seoul, dropping a Leuven source specimen or one paired Seoul participant from the destination summary. No joint deletions and no refitting or rule selection. Summary metrics are gene MAE gain relative to zero and fractions of deletions with positive gain. A complete Leuven participant-to-specimen map is unavailable. These are influence diagnostics conditional on the measured data, not confidence intervals, prospective validation, or additional independent cohorts. Do not use a tooth as a biological replicate when assessing uncertainty.
