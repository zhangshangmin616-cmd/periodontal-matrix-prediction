External persistence transfer from the Nanjing reference to Leuven periodontal whole-tissue expression

This protocol fixes a new analysis of public cohorts previously inspected in the project. It precedes the numerical computation of the Leuven day-7-to-day-28 forecast errors. The source-derived linear persistence coefficient 0.23238716799216566 was fitted in Nanjing to day 3 versus day 7 responses by ordinary least squares without an intercept, excluding all 101 target genes. Use exactly this coefficient for each gene without refitting. The validation contrasts are from the Leuven/Zhao PRJNA1136245 cohort, anatomically unsorted by force side. Each time's unloaded baseline must be drawn from its own batch proxy. The 19 day-7 treated and five batch-4 control specimens define the predictors. The 17 day-28 treated and 22 batch-3 control specimens define the observations. The tested time and loading regimes differ across studies. Leuven previously contributed other endpoints in this project. There are no matched longitudinal individuals in the published sample mapping.

Primary estimand: for each of the fixed 10,806 genes, calculate CPM with the entire run's depth, apply log2(CPM+1) to each specimen, and take treated mean minus the matched batch control mean separately at each time. For each gene predict its day-28 shift as a fixed coefficient times its day-7 shift. Compare absolute prediction error against coefficients 0 (no change), 1 (full carry-over), 0.25 and 0.5. Give genes equal weight. Report MAE on the nominated 101 and the full 10,806 background, for every coefficient. Secondary estimand: average CPM first for each of the four specimen groups and then use log2(mean CPM+1); repeat all model and comparator scores with otherwise identical rules. Provide descriptive signed bias, no per-gene or pseudo-patient significance tests. No comparison of outcomes will be used to choose coefficients or restrict genes. A better fit than the zero and persistence baselines alone does not establish an advantage over one-quarter persistence. This RNA-scale comparison is distinct from a percentile-rank pathway score and from the direct intrusion/extrusion contrasts in Chengdu.
