# Temporal and anatomical specificity of expression transfer

This retrospective extension compares a published human axial day-10 contrast against a prediction using (i) no change, (ii) the matching or opposite side of the day-7 atlas, and (iii) the same or opposite axial loading arm at day 3. We also report the simple average of both day-3 clinical arms as a direction-agnostic comparator. The Chengdu source includes a shared set of untreated teeth, so day-3 and day-10 estimates are correlated even when treated groups differ. This is a same-study comparator with an information advantage. This comparison cannot identify an independent forecast accuracy or attribute cross-time similarity entirely to persistence.

Use the fixed 101 ECM panel and, separately, the 10,806-gene fixed background. For each group of genes require all four published time-by-arm coefficients and both atlas coefficients so every method and arm is scored on exactly the same genes. No filter on significance, effect size or agreement. Report excluded genes, absolute error with equal gene weights, and mean signed error. Score each day-10 target separately, retain the two arms as correlated comparisons and examine a difference of arm coefficients as a sensitivity analysis. Subtraction of two published coefficients estimates a descriptive difference and is distinct from the directly fitted arm-to-arm contrast. Atlas fold changes come from the common baseline profile, fixed RNA fractions and the five named cell groups. No parameters are fitted on the clinical targets.
