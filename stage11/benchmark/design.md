# Day-3 to day-7 comparison on the human loading atlas

The two scored predictions are retrospective library-level forecasts in the original atlas. Use CP and the corresponding day-3 library to form predictions. Use the two day-7 libraries only for outcome measurement. The original day-7 data and a state-only panel analysis had already been examined. No coefficients, gene filters, or cutoffs will be selected using forecast performance. One library per condition makes uncertainty across humans inaccessible.

Compare no change, change of five within-group RNA profiles only, change of six RNA-mass proportions only, change of both (residual group expression profile fixed), and the ordinary carry-forward of the entire measured day-3 tissue profile. Cell group RNA-mass proportions are the fractions of captured UMIs, not cell counts. Use the fixed 101 target genes, the fixed 10,806-gene background and all 37,433 uniquely named atlas genes. The last set includes undetected genes. Each gene has equal weight within a set. Report absolute error of log2(CPM+1) changes from CP, and the direction of change with explicit tie counts. Retain both library-specific errors and the equal-weight average. Also compare the direct pressure-minus-tension response, which cancels a common CP baseline, and reverse the day-3 side labels as a descriptive specificity check.

For aggregate ranks, preserve the earlier convention of applying a smoothed relative-expression multiplier to the untreated abundance and then reranking. Separately score direct reconstructed expression in both per-gene and rank endpoints. For ranks the background always contains 10,806 genes. Its aggregate mean rank is fixed by construction, so evaluate both the change in the 101-target mean percentile and each gene's percentile change. Compute midranks using exact integer/rational quantities to retain ties.
