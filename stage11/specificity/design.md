# Expression-matched comparison of periodontal gene sets

This analysis was specified after seeing the historical 101-gene aggregate results. Use the stage 5 state-only model, baseline-calibrated six-class RNA weights and equal pressure/tension contributions for each external control. Quantify each gene's observed and predicted change in percentile points within the identical 10,806-gene expression background. Retain the historical contrast unit of each study. Kim contributes 11 paired participants at three weeks, while Zhao contributes one batch-matched contrast at day 7 and another at day 28. Use day 7 atlas changes at each time.

For each contrast, compare (i) the absolute error in the average shift of the nominated genes and (ii) the arithmetic mean of gene-level absolute errors. Average the resulting errors across individuals for Kim, keeping days separate for Zhao. Compare the fixed 101 genes with 10,000 equally sized sets sampled exclusively from the genes marked outside_ecm in the fixed reference. Choose the same number as the 101 panel in each of ten deciles of average-ranked untreated atlas expression. Keep tied expression values in the same bin. Freeze the pool, bin membership and random draws across all three tests. Within a draw sample without replacement, across draws allow reuse. Summarise the spread of gains over zero and the portion equalling or exceeding the target. This is a gene-set comparison conditional on the data, independent of any inferential claim concerning patients. Use PCG64 seed 20261124.
