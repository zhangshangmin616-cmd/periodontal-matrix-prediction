# Which of the frozen genes have an independent day-7 root-side RNA measurement?

The search of 25 November 2026 used NCBI PubMed. The 17 queries covered the 101 fixed symbols, approved gene names and selected aliases in titles and abstracts. Each query also required wording about tooth movement, periodontal tissues and either clinical recruitment or side sampling. We left time and species out of the query so that eligibility could be judged from the actual methods. The 48 distinct records have an individual disposition in `screened.tsv`. This is one search route. A gene name may appear only in an unindexed table. Separate repository and citation routes are recorded alongside this directory.

`assays.tsv` lists the biological targets from five public reports of human day-7 tissue by root side or mechanical zone. Reports are mapped separately. Patient overlap is possible. The root-side designation in the Masson thesis is taken from its mechanical-zone table. `coverage.tsv` gives one row for every fixed gene, with a distinct column for use as a PCR reference. The COL-I assay mapped to COL1A1 and the TGF-beta assay to TGFB1 in the Garlet report. A companion primer-to-transcript mapping is in `stage10/human/garlet07_assay_map.tsv`. Protein staining, genes mentioned in discussion and RNA expressed in other species never contribute to the measured-target column. Blanks indicate missing measurements or unresolved numerical comparisons. All assay directions are group-level summaries.

Run `python stage16/primary/screen_records.py` and `python stage16/primary/coverage.py` to recreate the two tables offline. The exact NCBI queries and live links are in `pubmed_queries.tsv`; bibliographic metadata are in `bibliography.json`. Live query counts may change.

`python stage16/primary/gene_table.py` combines this crosswalk with the two fixed ACTB-relative log predictions, and with the original prediction and observation for unsplit Leuven tissue. The Chinese-language spreadsheet-readable `gene_evidence.csv` uses six decimal places and a UTF-8 byte order mark. Empty observations mean no reported value. A zero for ACTB on the log scale arises from using ACTB as its own denominator. The Leuven columns are percentile-rank changes on a different scale. The two clinical endpoints cannot be subtracted from one another.
