# Discovery by citation links

Search date: 25 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai). Search Europe PMC's public cited-by and references endpoints from the 2007 and 2008 Garlet papers, the related Menezes study, the 2008 Anastasi and 2019 Militi human periodontal ligament studies, the clinical day-7 progenitor study by Chen, and the single-cell source atlas. Retain unique bibliographic records and linkage direction. Prior audits are particularly important for these seeds: they do not represent newly sourced independent studies. Check title-selected primary candidates, relevant full text and publicly linked supplements. The bibliographic search covers only records indexed by this service and cannot prove nonexistence. Candidates identified by parallel keyword searches may supplement the chain.

The target design requires another human in vivo 7-day periodontal ligament experiment with the pressure and tension tissue actually sampled separately, independent of the original atlas and with RNA measurements for the fixed 101 genes. Test panels with genuine measurements of a subset may be documented but cannot supply whole-panel validation. A force applied in a known direction, two contralateral teeth, days of culture after extraction, separate assays in rodents, gingival crevicular fluid, or an author's literature-derived target-gene list cannot substitute for a direct measurement by root surface. Assess assay-to-target overlap using the frozen target gene list irrespective of the direction of the measured outcome.

Also queried both bibliographic directions for the same seven seeds via OpenAlex. Stored one record per seed, direction and linked bibliographic record in each provider. Within-provider unique ID counts describe bibliographic coverage. The two provider counts must not be added to claim a count of independent papers. Did not full-text screen every bibliographic link. For the abbreviated primary-source audit, cross-checked previous project results, selectively read clinically relevant candidates and targeted Portuguese, Spanish and English searches by tooth surface, time, cohort and assay. Selected follow-up terms included premolars, compression, tension, buccal, palatal, ligamento periodontal, lado de pressão, lado de presión, osteocalcina, laminina, TGFB1, ACTA2, THBS1 and the corresponding factor names.
