# Day-7 human periodontal ligament studies found through literature citations

Searched on 25 November 2026. No newly verified independent cohort in the citation search supplied human day-7 RNA amplitudes for all 101 genes separately from sampled pressure and tension sides. The best previously located tissue evidence on the frozen panel remains a single biological outcome in Garlet et al. The 2014 Bogotá conference abstract supplies previously untracked bilateral sampling detail but used 8 or 15 days. All three assayed genes fell outside the frozen panel. The 2016 Bogotá journal study did measure the two root sides at seven days, although its two outcome genes also fall outside the panel. Participant overlap between the conference study and the journal study has not been established. [Conference proceedings, pp. 23–25](https://acfo.edu.co/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/ACFO-Mem2014_xxv_EncuentroInvest.pdf) · [2016 study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4727488/)

## Garlet et al., Figure 1, printed page 357

The primary report describes seven loaded subjects (separate root sides) and five untreated controls. TGF-β expression, mapped to TGFB1 with the authors’ Table 1 assay in the stage-10 audit, was higher on each loaded side than in controls. Each side-to-control comparison had P < 0.001. The pressure–tension comparison did not reach significance. The figure legend identifies each plotted point as the triplicate technical mean of a biological sample normalized to β-actin. ACTB was the reference assay. The accessible machine-readable primary text and legend contain no numerical group centers or fold changes for this gene. These data establish two signs for a single gene and leave the ordering of the two loaded sides unresolved. [Authors’ full text and Figure 1 legend](https://www.academia.edu/82408528/Cytokine_expression_pattern_in_compression_and_tension_sides_of_the_periodontal_ligament_during_orthodontic_tooth_movement_in_humans) · [Indexed primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17850423/)

The publisher landing page exposes the abstract. OpenAlex lists Wiley and PubMed without a public PDF link. The author-shared page and its archived public HTML supply the article text and figure legend plus a first-page thumbnail. The numerical panel is on the third printed page. A machine-readable numeric supplement or publicly accessible image from which to measure those points was not verified here. The related Brazilian meeting abstracts report directional results, have overlapping investigators and no cross-publication participant linkage. Neither establishes numerical group effects for Figure 1 or another independent day-7 cohort. ## Reproduction and scope

`search.py` queries OpenAlex by default and Europe PMC with `--epmc`. Their responses are frozen in `links.tsv` and `epmc_links.tsv`. The OpenAlex file contains 1,063 links and 959 unique work IDs; the Europe PMC file contains 715 links and 643 unique nonempty source/ID pairs, plus two reference rows without an ID. Both sets contain references and citations for the same seven seeds and have considerable bibliographic overlap. These numbers count database links, not fully screened trials or independent participants. `screened.tsv` records 15 selected primary-source adjudications from citation or keyword follow-up. Previous project checks help to distinguish novel leads from known sources. `protocol.md` details the selection question and bounds of the search. Original-study links and assay units are preserved in the adjudication file.
