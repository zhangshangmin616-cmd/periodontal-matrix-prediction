# What does a whole-root RNA measurement establish about root-side predictions?

Use the previously evaluated Leuven day-seven expression-only model with cell-group RNA weights held fixed, the original five untreated control profiles, all 10,806 background genes and the frozen 101 targets. This is a diagnostic of the measurement design using existing predictions and outcomes. Predict the two root sides for each untreated specimen before any mixing or ranking. Compare three assignments: original side labels, exchanged labels, and the same equal-weight linear RNA profile assigned to both sides. In all three, evaluate the same pooled clinical endpoint under the recorded 50:50 working assumption, using within-sample percentile ranks and the observed group contrast. Report exact per-gene as well as panel-level agreement of the pooled forecasts. Separately quantify how the predicted side contrast changes under the three assignments. The unsplit clinical observations do not measure this contrast. No donor counts or performance estimates are inferred from the equivalence. For a known unequal mixing weight, two identical copies of the corresponding weighted profile remain an observationally equivalent alternative with a zero between-side contrast. This is not a fitted anatomical weight.
