# Public registry audit for human day-7 root-side RNA

On 25 November 2026 (Asia/Shanghai) we looked for RNA measurements from human periodontal ligament after seven days of clinical orthodontic loading, with separately identified pressure- and tension-side specimens. An independent test of the fixed panel also requires identifiable measurements for the 101 genes. A test using its original expression percentiles needs the ranking background; a cell-group forecast additionally needs measurement of that group. The public records screened here supplied no second series meeting these criteria. Queries did not require an untreated comparator for discovery, so a usable direct pressure-minus-tension contrast would also have been retained.

## Search and deduplication

The NCBI Sequence Read Archive (SRA) was searched directly, including submissions without a GEO series. Three human-organism queries searched (1) orthodontic or tooth movement; (2) ligament/membrane and local root-side terms; and (3) ligament/membrane and seven-day terms. They returned 556, 4 and 4 experiment UIDs, respectively, totalling **558 distinct experiment records from 14 BioProjects**. A matching SRA experiment is not a complete cohort or an independent patient. The broad query can match wording used for recruitment or indications for extraction. All 14 projects and their reasons for inclusion or exclusion are listed in `projects.tsv`. The two targeted queries carry an Entrez warning that the quoted synonym “periodontal membrane” did not match as an indexed phrase. We recorded query translations and warnings. A study indexed under other words can be missed. [NCBI SRA](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra/)

All four root-side hits belong to the reference study. The date query yielded the two day-7 libraries from that atlas and two D7 libraries from a separate study, **PRJNA390189 / GSE99958**. The latter are mRNA and miRNA from day 7 of osteogenic differentiation in culture. A second easily confused result, PRJNA1099930, measured RNA responses in cultured human periodontal ligament stem cells exposed to mechanical and magnetic stimuli. Its in vivo compression-side experiment was in rats. [GSE99958 study design](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE99958) · [Magnetic-field study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11059396/)

Five DataCite queries returned **85, 18, 6, 8 and 0 records**, respectively. We combined three dataset searches using orthodontic-force or ligament terms, one search allowing any registered resource type with both side terms and ligament, and one exact related-DOI search for the Tu and Spitz papers. The union comprised 99 distinct DOIs with 57 distinct title strings. These are identifiers and titles, not 57 verified studies. The same data can appear under a versioned DOI and a concept DOI, multiple supplementary items, or several records with differing titles. The recorded returns include Figshare, Zenodo, Mendeley Data, university repositories and publisher supplements. The additional BioStudies queries combining both sides with periodontal ligament returned 0 and 5 entries, all five were S-EPMC publication-index entries. They did not establish five expression-matrix deposits. A zero publication-DOI linkage does not establish that no differently named deposit exists. Each URL, returned identifier and pagination total is archived in the respective JSON. [DataCite REST API](https://api.datacite.org/dois) · [BioStudies search API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/api/v1/search)

## Source-study positive control

Every GEO specimen in GSE327337 was matched to its GEO title, BioSample and SRA experiment, then independently to its ENA experiment and public run. The five GEO-labelled specimens map to five runs under **PRJNA1450544 / SRP690124**. The two databases thus identify the same specimens. Nothing in the crosswalk establishes that each run represents one identifiable human donor. ## Additional archive leads and unresolved records

* **University of Southern California Digital Library.** DataCite has three molecular-expression thesis records. Their metadata give a title but no abstract, named creator or sample manifest. Direct requests to all three university landing pages returned HTTP 403 in this audit. An Internet Archive title search did not locate the texts. We therefore leave the individual thesis designs unresolved. A related investigator report from the same university, with Maung, Reyna and Beom Moon as co-investigators, documents a Wistar rat experiment using left and right molars and rat microarrays on days 1–5. The report identifies the model for that funded project; the links available here do not establish that every thesis uses exactly those samples. [Orthodontic tooth movement or root resorption thesis](https://doi.org/10.25549/usctheses-ouc113987625) · [Identification of genes thesis](https://doi.org/10.25549/usctheses-ouc113992650) · [Early events thesis](https://doi.org/10.25549/usctheses-ouc113998t8i) · [Principal investigator's report, p. 1](https://www.aaofoundation.net/ResourceFiles/pdf/zeichner-david-2001-final-report.pdf)
* **Figshare molecular studies.** In the UCHL1 study, human ligament cells received mechanical stress in culture and in vivo orthodontic movement was examined in rats. In the SOST/sclerostin study the mapped root sides belonged to mice at days 0, 1, 5 and 10; human cells were treated in vitro. A further miRNA-expression supplement reports profiles after stretching cultured cells. These records identify real public assays but do not provide a human day-7 root-side mRNA matrix. [UCHL1 deposit](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.26893468) · [SOST collection](https://doi.org/10.25384/sage.c.4123454) · [Mechanical-force microRNA supplement](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.4541032.v1)
* **Other recent mechanistic studies.** The human fibroblast profiling in the H3K18la paper used 24-hour cyclic stretch; its referenced in vivo periodontal fibroblast single-cell data are murine. A separate Nebraska transcriptomic time course included a day-7 pressure-side sample but assayed rat alveolar bone. [H3K18la study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41368-026-00465-0) · [Nebraska primary report](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36891648/)

Counts for different APIs must not be summed as independent datasets. ENA was used to verify the run-level linkage after the SRA search, not to infer absence from wildcards. Previous work separately screened GEO, NGDC, OMIX, CELLxGENE and clinical repositories. This module focuses on the additional SRA, DataCite and BioStudies queries and the exact run crosswalk.

## Reproducibility

`python stage16/registry/check.py` verifies the archived metadata offline, including query totals, experiment-set union, the project-level disposition list and exact source-sample relationships. `python stage16/registry/collect.py --out fresh_registry` repeats retrieval from the public APIs using the Python standard library; optional `--resources sra datacite biostudies mirror` limits the retrieval. New public records can change the totals in a later live search.
