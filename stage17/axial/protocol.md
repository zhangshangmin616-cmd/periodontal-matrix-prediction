# Endpoint comparison in previously retrieved human axial loading data

The Chengdu cohort contributed gene-level clinical checks during earlier stages of this study. This module adds a paired examination of absolute panel-mean error and mean individual-gene error on the same scale. Use the four pre-existing fixed-share tissue prediction vectors generated from the separate day-3 and day-7 single-cell atlas before extraction of the four HOTM treatment-versus-control contrasts. Apply day 3 to day 3 and the previously chosen day 7 to day 10, with extrusion and intrusion mapped to tension- and pressure-dominant conditions. Use all 101 fixed target symbols, retaining missing measurements and all published numerical coefficients regardless of significance.

For each direction and time calculate the mean signed observed and predicted effects, the absolute errors of those means, and mean absolute gene errors versus zero. An incomplete arm has no directly calculable full-panel mean. Report the complete-case scores for it and, separately, the interval of permissible full-panel error gains. With unrestricted missing observations, the gain for the full-panel mean has closure ±|mean forecast|. For individual genes each unobserved effect contributes a gain in [−|forecast|,+|forecast|]. For complete arms assess robustness to deleting one gene at a time. These are gene-influence checks, not donor-level uncertainty. Four comparisons share controls and do not constitute four replications.

The outcome is a group-level, application-reported log₂ differential expression coefficient. It is on a different scale from the clinical percentile-rank endpoint, which requires expression measured for every background gene within each individual specimen. The target genes, predictors and four contrasts were previously specified; the new side-by-side summaries are retrospective.
