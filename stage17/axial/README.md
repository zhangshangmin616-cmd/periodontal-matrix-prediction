# Extension to an existing clinical study

The 31 tooth labels in the Chengdu HOTM resource include eight unloaded controls. The four archived contrasts compare the controls with four extruded or four intruded specimens at each of days 3 and 10. These are tissue profiles from the apical two thirds of the root, sampled around the circumference. Duration is an exact match to the atlas only at day 3. The new quantity in this reanalysis is the absolute error in the mean of the frozen 101 gene coefficients, evaluated alongside the original mean absolute gene error. The comparison has no donor-level precision estimate. [Zhang et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/ocr.70103) · [HOTM public tool](https://zhangxiaoqi67.shinyapps.io/HOTM/)

The study reports group differences as log₂ coefficients from the HOTM application. For its day-3 extrusion arm (101/101), the model reduced panel error from 0.1024 to 0.0711 and increased individual-gene error from 0.3570 to 0.5059. The two day-10 arms had greater errors than zero for both endpoints. Intrusion at day 3 omitted SGCZ. Among the 100 measured targets its panel error rose from 0.0020 to 0.0115. Across all 101, allowed panel gains range from −0.0018 to +0.0018, with negative full-panel gene gain for every possible missing value. Single-gene deletions refer to genes included in the measured subset.

The paper cites HRA010672 for sequence data. The GSA-Human landing record, the public download route, the public individual-expression heatmap and indexed article supplements were checked. We could not verify a downloadable library-level matrix or open individual files sufficient for the common within-sample percentile calculation. The authors’ public, unthresholded group tables contain overall means and fold changes. Those quantities do not supply expression ranks within each tooth. The GSA access designation for this specific accession remains unverified. [GSA-Human accession](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gsa-human/browse/HRA010672)

From the extracted package run `python stage17/axial/score.py` then `python stage17/axial/check.py`. The inputs are the original four HOTM tables and the frozen `stage8/axial_log2_multipliers.tsv.gz`. Only the three fully observed comparators have point-identified 101-gene errors for both endpoints.
