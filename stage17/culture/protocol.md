# Wuhan tension culture

Study GSE312900 entered previous literature screens as a human culture, which made it ineligible for earlier root-surface clinical validation. Its expression outcomes have not previously contributed to the frozen atlas or 101-target benchmark. The public sample titles identify sh-control and sh-PIEZO1 conditions exposed to 0, 24 or 48 hours of tension, with three samples per condition. Evaluate the nine sh-control samples at both durations, always relative to the three shared 0-hour control samples. Primary contrast is 24 h, the earlier observation. The 48-h result examines the later duration in the same study. Treat all observations as library-group contrasts. Donor matching and simultaneously harvested unloaded controls at 24 and 48 h are undocumented.

Use the inherited day-3 tension atlas multipliers without amplitude fitting or time adjustment. The primary predictor changes cell-group expression at the source's fixed RNA shares. Use the fibroblast-like predictor as a secondary comparator and score the zero vector on every endpoint. Apply the multipliers to each untreated library's linear abundance, rank within the mapped subset of the original 10,806-gene universe with average ties on 0–100, then subtract its baseline ranks and average. Observed changes are differences in mean within-library percentile ranks between loaded and baseline groups. Score the absolute error after averaging across the 101 fixed genes, and the mean of 101 absolute errors. Coverage, number of background genes, directions of both gains, and single-library deletion results accompany the full result. Do not treat genes or technical runs as participants. Never drop a target for being zero.

Use NCBI's uniformly processed count files if accessible for all runs in the nine libraries, summing counts of technical runs before scoring. Report the authors' published sample-level FPKM matrix as a processing sensitivity check, or as the primary input if the former are unavailable.
