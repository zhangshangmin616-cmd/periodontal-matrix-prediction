# Independent 12-hour tensile culture submitted as GSE106887

GEO describes human PDLSCs derived from the mid-root surface of healthy impacted third molars from people aged 18–20. Cells underwent 12 hours of 10% equibiaxial stretch at 1 Hz. The series contains one normal library (GSM2856840/SRR6291210), one 12-hour stretch library (GSM2856842/SRR6291212) and a separate 7-day osteogenic induction library. The assay contributor is Fulan Wei and the GEO contact was at Zhejiang University. Details of the stretch contrast come from the submitted study and sample records. The paper linked from GEO concerns osteogenic differentiation. No individual donor-level evaluation is possible for the one-library-per-arm RNA contrast. [Series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE106887) · [Stretched library](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2856842) · [Sequence archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJNA418326)

NCBI uniform counts recover all 101 targets on the original 10,806-gene background. The observed panel mean changed by −1.420 percentile points. The fixed-share tissue model predicted +0.309. Thus panel error was 1.729 versus 1.420 for zero, and individual-gene error was 8.330 versus 6.370. The fibroblast-like comparator also increased both errors. This single contrast provides an example where the proposed aggregate advantage does not transfer.

The authors also provide a Cufflinks table of genomic loci and assembled transcript assignments, with expression columns PDLZC (normal), J12h (stretch), and D7d (osteogenic induction). An unambiguous one-row-per-exact-symbol mapping retains 8,250 background genes and 80 of the 101 targets. The table for those 80 is a secondary completeness check. On that subset, tissue panel errors were 2.753 versus 2.459 for zero and gene errors 9.511 versus 8.044. Counts on the full panel supply the primary score.
