# Conditional deletion checks for the target-informed diagnostic

The full-sample oracle results were known when this check was added. For Leuven, delete each loaded specimen once at each time, using the original five or 22 untreated specimens and the same fixed aggregate forecast. Reconstruct each target outcome from the full 10,806-gene library rank. The atlas forecast depends on untreated specimens and their calibration weights. Removing only loaded specimens leaves these inputs intact. This check therefore describes influence among measured exposed specimens, conditional on the full untreated baseline. No re-pairing is attempted. For Seoul, remove each of the 11 participant outcome and prediction vectors in turn while keeping the other originally fitted forecasts fixed. Recompute participant-first and group-mean-first loss. For each deletion, re-optimise the full amplitude family and the family that holds the predicted panel mean fixed. Report the attainable gain over zero or, for the latter family, over the matching uniformly predicted mean. These are full-access outcome diagnostics; the ranges are not confidence intervals.
