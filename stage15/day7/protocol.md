# Seven-day ex vivo human progenitor cell measurements

Use all directly labelled human ex vivo qPCR targets measured in progenitor cultures isolated after seven days of tooth loading in Chen et al. The authors' abstract, methods and narrative results have been seen. Record analytical choices before reading numeric bars or atlas predictions. Scope is restricted to clinically loaded human cells and their human controls, including individually labelled targets in main Fig. 4d and Supplementary Fig. S3a. Exclude co-cultured monocytes, mice, rats, blots, cytokine secretion, and derived ratios that would count a measured gene twice.

Calculate log2(loaded bar height / untreated bar height) for each gene, using the same axis baseline and both bar tops. Use the printed axis label to establish the reference gene and, if it is identified, reference the atlas prediction to that gene on the same scale. Estimate digitisation sensitivity to placement of the baseline and bar edges, separate from biological uncertainty. Archive image locations, intensity/geometry rules and the source hashes. The numerical results describe published bars. They cannot reconstruct individual donors' paired effects.

Compare with previously frozen day-7 tissue multipliers at fixed cell-group RNA shares and with the fibroblast-like profile alone derived from the same atlas counts. For sides not identified in the human ex vivo measurements use an equal-weight average of pressure and tension multipliers on the linear scale. Repeat at both pure sides as sensitivity checks. Mix the target and housekeeping fold factors separately before calculating the log-ratio. Score all eligible targets, with gene MAE and panel-mean error at each side fraction, both compared with zero. Evaluate the range across the complete mixing interval if feasible. Document tissue source, preparation, clinical design, comparability of the two jaws, gene-panel membership and any contradiction in the source.
