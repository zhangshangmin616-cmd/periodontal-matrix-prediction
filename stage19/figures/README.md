# Source data for the main figures

Each CSV contains the gene-level measurements and forecasts underlying one figure. All changes are loaded minus baseline in within-library expression-rank percentile points. The constant `zero` is the zero-change forecast. Running `python plot_figures.py` next to the three CSVs regenerates the PDF and PNG files with Python 3, NumPy and Matplotlib. The vector PDFs preserve text as embedded TrueType fonts and are 177.5 mm wide.

| File | Observation unit | Fields and origin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Figure_1_source_data.csv | One clinical group contrast × 101 genes | `observed` and `atlas` are from `stage13/transfer/forecasts.tsv`, Leuven day 7. `atlas_shared_mean` assigns the arithmetic mean of the 101 atlas changes to every gene. The target observations derive from BioProject PRJNA1136245. The atlas derives from GSE327337. |
| Figure_2_source_data.csv | Three culture group contrasts × 101 genes, plus five paired held-out donor contrasts × 101 genes | The Wuhan atlas columns come from `stage17/wuhan_counts/targets.tsv` (tissue/rank, GSE312900). The second stretch series uses `stage17/zhejiang/targets.tsv` (NCBI_counts/tissue, GSE106887). The five paired predictions are calculated from `stage18/transfer/hong_kong_donor_changes.tsv` (GSE173891). For each held-out donor and gene, `predicted` averages the four other donors’ changes. The library counts refer to the unit being scored. The Wuhan contrasts reuse the same baseline libraries and the Hong Kong folds reuse training donors. |
| Figure_3_source_data.csv | Three culture group contrasts × 101 genes | The observed and predicted shifts come from `stage18/transfer/genes.tsv`, with a common 10,805-gene rank background. The `centred_amplitude` is the full five-donor source fit in `stage18/deviations/training.tsv`. Writing m for the target atlas forecast mean and p for its gene-specific prediction, the calibrated value is m + amplitude × (p − m). The six methods are zero, measured, measured_shared_mean, atlas, atlas_calibrated and atlas_shared_mean. |

The three target contrasts in Figure 2a use the complete 10,806-gene background. Figure 3 recalculates these contrasts on the 10,805 genes also measurable in Hong Kong. All 101 targets are present in both comparisons. One non-target gene (RUNX2) accounts for the difference in background size. Slight numerical differences can therefore appear when rounded to three decimal places. In Figure 3 both shared-shift rules preserve their respective source means. At the individual-gene level they can change the error.

For a vector of per-gene residuals (prediction minus observation), the two plotted losses are the absolute value of the average residual and the average of the absolute residuals. Figure 2b computes each loss within a held-out donor and averages with equal donor weights. No donor confidence intervals are inferred from the number of genes, study libraries or scoring folds.

The original public records are [the Nanjing atlas](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE327337), [Leuven clinical specimens](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1136245), [Hong Kong cultures](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE173891), [Wuhan cultures](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE312900) and [the single-pair stretch series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE106887).

In the original research archive all stage paths in the table are relative to the archive root. To regenerate the figure CSVs, preserve that layout and put `assemble_data.py` in `stage19/figures` before running it. Its output can then be rendered independently by `plot_figures.py`.
