# Main figure legends

**Figure 1. Accuracy of a clinical forecast at two levels of aggregation.** a, Each point is one of 101 matrix genes in the Leuven day-7 contrast (19 loaded specimens and five untreated specimens from the same sequencing batch). The horizontal coordinate is the atlas prediction at fixed cell-group RNA shares, averaged equally over pressure and tension. The vertical coordinate is the observed rank shift. The dashed line marks perfect agreement. The prediction was closer to the observation than zero change for 41 genes and farther away for 60. b, Panel error is the absolute difference between the predicted and observed means. Gene error is the average absolute difference for the 101 genes. Assigning the atlas mean shift to every gene preserves the panel score. All changes and errors are expressed in percentile points (pp) on the 10,806-gene background.

**Figure 2. The ordering of panel and gene errors depends on the forecast and the experiment.** a, A day-3 tension-side atlas forecast was applied to human periodontal ligament cultures. Gain equals zero-change error minus forecast error in expression percentile points (pp). Wuhan contributed three libraries at each time, with both contrasts using the same three 0-h libraries. GSE106887 had one baseline and one stretched library. b, Each line connects the two gains for one held-out Hong Kong donor after 12 h of stretch. Each gene's forecast is the mean paired change in the four other donors. The five held-out donors gained 0.302 points on average for individual genes and lost 0.053 points for the panel mean. Diamonds show these equal-weight donor averages. All scores use 101 targets. The rank background is 10,806 genes in a and 10,805 in b.

**Figure 3. Cross-laboratory prediction with gene-specific and shared changes.** Absolute errors in expression percentile points (pp) are shown for the panel mean (a) and averaged over individual genes (b). Each cell is one group contrast. Shading compares the displayed error with zero-change error in the same column and panel. The Hong Kong forecast transfers each gene’s mean 12-h response across five donor pairs. Its shared-shift counterpart assigns the panel mean to every gene. The atlas forecast uses the day-3 tension-side profiles. Calibration retains the predicted panel mean and multiplies each gene’s deviation from it by 0.0482, fitted in Hong Kong. The shared atlas forecast sets these deviations to zero. Both Wuhan time points share three baseline libraries and each has three loaded libraries. GSE106887 has one library per condition. All methods use the same 101 targets and 10,805 background genes.
